About NetraMitra

Netra means eye and Mitra means friend. NetraMitra started from personal experience. After four eye surgeries, the founder often faced small problems with no quick way to know if they were serious, and had to travel to the hospital even for minor issues.

NetraMitra gives patients limited, general guidance so they feel more informed and less anxious between hospital visits.

✅ What we do

  • Share general eye-care information from trusted sources
  • Help you understand common symptoms
  • Point out warning signs
  • Remind you to see an ophthalmologist

❌ What we do not do

  • Give a medical diagnosis
  • Replace your doctor
  • Prescribe or change medicines
  • Handle emergencies
Emergency? Sudden vision loss, severe pain, a chemical splash or an eye injury needs immediate medical care. Go to the nearest hospital.
