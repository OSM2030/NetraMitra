NetraMitra

Your eye care companion. Netra means eye and Mitra means friend.

NetraMitra is a website that gives basic guidance to people with eye problems, before and after eye surgery. The idea is simple: know what is normal, and know when to see your doctor.

Why I'm building this

I have had multiple eye surgeries. Many times after surgery I had small problems like redness or something in my eye, and I could not tell if it was serious. Sometimes the internet did not help, so I had to travel to my ophthalmologist, and often the reason turned out to be very small. I want to build something that helps patients in that situation.

Important

This project gives general information only. It is not a medical diagnosis and it does not replace a doctor. Always visit an ophthalmologist for anything serious. In an emergency (sudden vision loss, severe pain, chemical splash, eye injury), go to the nearest hospital.

This is currently a demo project, and the guidance content has not been reviewed by a doctor yet.

Planned sections
Normal eye problems - upload a photo and answer a few questions about redness, eye flu, irritation or something in the eye, and get general advice.
Pre-surgery help - upload the pre-operative slip and medicine slip to know what to prepare.
Post-surgery help - upload post-operative documents to follow recovery and know the warning signs.
